excerpt: "A sample reminder to be considerate when using chargers at hotels, parks, and other destinations."

Destination chargers are often shared amenities. A little courtesy can make charging easier for everyone.

- Follow posted parking and charging instructions.
- Move your vehicle when charging is complete if the space is needed by another driver.
- Keep cables tidy and avoid blocking nearby access.
- Respect any access limits for customers, guests, or permit holders.
- Contact the site if instructions or charger status are unclear.
