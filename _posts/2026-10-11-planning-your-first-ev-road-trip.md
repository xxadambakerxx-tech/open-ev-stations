## Start with a comfortable range

Plan charging stops before the battery gets low. Leave room for weather, hills, traffic, and detours, all of which can affect how far a vehicle travels between charges.

## Check the stop details

Before setting out, confirm the station's connector type, access hours, and any fees. Destination chargers may be limited to customers or overnight guests, so check with the location if the listing is unclear.

## Keep a backup in mind

Identify another charging option near a planned stop. A backup can help if a charger is occupied, unavailable, or outside your vehicle's usable range.
