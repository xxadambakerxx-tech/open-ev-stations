## Check the connector before you go

Charging stations may offer different connector standards. Check your vehicle's supported connectors and compare them with the station listing before planning a stop.

## Know which charging level you need

Level 2 equipment is commonly used for longer stops, while DC fast charging is designed for quicker charging where supported. Actual charging speed can depend on the vehicle, the charger, and operating conditions.

## Confirm compatibility

Some vehicles can use an adapter for certain connector types. Follow your vehicle manufacturer's guidance and confirm the equipment is approved for your vehicle.
