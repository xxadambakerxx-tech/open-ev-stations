Use this sample checklist to verify the post page layout and the homepage feed links.

- Confirm the charger is available to you and check its access hours.
- Match the connector to your vehicle or adapter.
- Check the location's current pricing and any parking requirements.
- Keep the station address handy in case you need to contact the site.

Station information can change. Confirm current details with the charging location before you travel.
