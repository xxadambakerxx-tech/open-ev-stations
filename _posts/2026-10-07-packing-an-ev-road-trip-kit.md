This sample checklist adds another item to the article feed.

- The charging cable and adapters supported by your vehicle
- A phone cable and vehicle-compatible charging setup
- A tire pressure gauge and the vehicle's roadside assistance information
- Water and weather-appropriate essentials for longer stops
- A saved copy of important trip details in case mobile coverage is limited

Choose equipment suitable for your vehicle and follow its owner's manual.
